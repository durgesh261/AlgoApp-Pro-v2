## 10-Step Deterministic Validation Pipeline

The `DecisionEngineService` orchestrates the following exact 10-step sequence prior to generating a decision state:

1. **Session Filter Engine**: Enforces exact UTC market overlap windows (Asia, London, NY) and weekend trading restrictions.
2. **Market Regime & Volatility Engine**: Validates ATR (14 vs 200) expansion ratios to block outliers and low-volatility compressions.
3. **Trend Validator**: Checks strict confluence between internal structure, swing structure, and the target trade direction.
4. **Zone Validator**: Evaluates zone freshness decay, multi-touch exhaustion rules, and explicit price boundary breaks.
5. **Market Structure Validator**: Requires Break of Structure (BOS) or Change of Character (CHoCH) events aligned in the trade direction.
6. **Liquidity Validator**: Ensures previous equal highs/lows are swept and validates Fair Value Gap (FVG) confluences.
7. **Risk Validator**: Blocks trades if the calculated Risk-to-Reward ratio is below 2.0, Daily Loss Limits are reached, or Max Drawdown thresholds are breached.
8. **Position Sizing Engine**: Calculates exact institutional position sizing derived dynamically from distance to Stop Loss and a 1.0% Account Risk parameter.
9. **Deterministic AI Confirmation Layer**: Calculates a strict numerical confidence score. No generative hallucination is permitted; decisions rely solely on evaluated structurals.
10. **Signal Deduplication Engine**: Rejects repeat entries on the same candle, duplicate zone entries, concurrent open positions, and enforces cooldown timers.

## Output Reproducibility

Every decision generates an `inputSnapshotHash` (SHA256) of the evaluated conditions (Indicators, Rules, Risk, Timing). This hash guarantees absolute reproducibility. If an identical market state is passed into the pipeline, the exact same decision state, reason codes, and position sizing will be returned.
